## What to Look For

When you're cited:
- **Which page** was cited? (homepage, records, a state guide, a near-city page)
- **What context** was the citation in? (definition, comparison, recommendation)
- **Was it a direct link** or just a brand mention?

When you're NOT cited:
- **Who was cited instead?** (record the specific site)
- **What type of content** was cited? (comparison table, FAQ, guide, listicle)
- **What would need to change** for you to be cited instead?
