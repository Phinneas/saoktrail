Desert Soak covers hot springs across the American Southwest — Utah, Nevada, and Arizona.

**Field Methodology**

Every guide on this site is the product of an actual visit. We record GPS coordinates, road conditions, water temperatures, parking capacity, and cell coverage on-site. High-traffic springs are revisited across seasons. Guides are reviewed quarterly and updated when conditions change.

**Editorial Independence**

Desert Soak is independently owned and operated. We do not accept sponsored posts, paid placements, or complimentary resort stays in exchange for coverage.
