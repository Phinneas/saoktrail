## Trip Overview

Colorado has more hot springs than any other state in the Lower 48, and this 4-day loop hits the highlights. You'll experience everything from primitive rock pools in forested canyons to developed infinity pools overlooking 14,000-foot peaks.

The route takes you from Steamboat Springs in the north, through the Sawatch Range in central Colorado, down to the dramatic San Juan Mountains in the southwest, and back through the Arkansas River Valley. Each night brings a new soaking experience and a new mountain town to explore.

## What Makes This Trip Special

- **Five distinct soaking experiences**: primitive, developed, resort, riverside, and clothing-optional
- **14er views**: soak with views of Mount Princeton, the Sneffels Range, and the Sawatch
- **Historic mining towns**: Ouray, Silverton, and Buena Vista offer history and charm
- **Year-round access**: all stops are on paved roads and accessible in winter
- **Flexible**: easy to extend with extra days in any location

## Planning Notes

- **Strawberry Park**: reservations required, clothing-optional after dark
- **Mount Princeton**: upper pools are free, lower pools require resort day pass
- **Ouray Hot Springs**: recently renovated, family-friendly, $20 day pass
- **Valley View**: clothing-optional, limited capacity, call ahead
- **Winter driving**: Monarch Pass can be snowy — check conditions
