## Trip Overview

The Northern Rockies Wilderness Soak is for adventurers who want to combine remote hot springs with some of the most dramatic landscapes in the American West. This 4-day trip takes you from Idaho's cliffside springs through Yellowstone's geothermal wonderland and into Montana's historic hot springs resorts.

The itinerary requires a high-clearance vehicle for some stops and includes camping in national forests and Yellowstone. The reward is soaking in pools that most travelers never see, from cascading cliffside springs to a natural hot spring-fed river inside a national park.

## What Makes This Trip Special

- **Yellowstone's Firehole River**: soak in a river warmed by geothermal features
- **Cliffside springs**: Kirkham's pools spill down a cliff above the Payette River
- **Historic Montana resort**: Chico Hot Springs has been operating since 1900
- **Ghost towns and mining history**: explore the old West along the route
- **Three states in four days**: Idaho, Wyoming, and Montana hot springs

## Planning Notes

- **Yellowstone entrance fee**: $35 per vehicle (7-day pass)
- **Firehole River**: only open in summer when water temps are safe — check at visitor center
- **Kirkham Hot Springs**: free, $5 campground fee, first-come first-served
- **Chico Hot Springs**: reservations recommended, historic rooms with shared baths
- **High-clearance needed**: Bonneville and some Idaho springs require dirt road access
- **Bear country**: carry bear spray in Yellowstone and national forest areas
