## Trip Overview

The Alaska Ferry Loop is the ultimate Southeast Alaska hot springs adventure. You'll travel the Inside Passage by ferry, soaking in natural hot springs that are accessible only by boat, in villages where cars are rare and the pace of life is set by the tide schedule.

This trip is unlike any other hot springs itinerary in the country. The springs are remote, the scenery is spectacular, and the journey itself — through glacial fjords and past breaching whales — is half the experience.

## What Makes This Trip Special

- **Ferry-dependent springs**: Baranof and Tenakee are only accessible by Alaska Marine Highway ferry
- **Car-free villages**: Tenakee Springs has no roads — just a boardwalk and a historic bathhouse
- **Wildlife everywhere**: humpback whales, orcas, brown bears, sea otters, and bald eagles
- **Cultural immersion**: these are working fishing villages, not tourist resorts
- **Minimal driving**: the ferry does the heavy lifting; you explore on foot

## Planning Notes

- **Alaska Marine Highway**: Book ferry reservations well in advance, especially July-August
- **Forest Service cabin**: Baranof cabin requires reservation through recreation.gov
- **Tenakee bathhouse**: $5 entry, open daylight hours, suits required
- **Weather**: Southeast Alaska is rainy — bring layers and waterproof gear
- **Food**: Limited dining options in Tenakee and Baranof — bring provisions
