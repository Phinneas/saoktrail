## Trip Overview

The Shasta Sacred Waters Loop explores the geothermal treasures of the southern Cascades, where volcanic activity creates natural mineral springs beneath the shadow of Mount Shasta. This 14,179-foot stratovolcano has been considered sacred by Native American tribes for thousands of years, and the springs that flow from its flanks carry that same spiritual significance.

The itinerary combines developed resort soaks with primitive wilderness pools, all within a short driving distance. You'll experience old-growth forests, waterfall hikes, and some of the most pristine mineral waters in California.

## What Makes This Trip Special

- **Sacred waters**: Mount Shasta's springs have been used for healing for millennia
- **Volcanic landscape**: drive through lava flows and volcanic formations
- **Varied soaking**: private cedar bathhouses, natural creek-side pools, and everything between
- **Year-round access**: most springs are accessible in all seasons
- **Small-town charm**: Mount Shasta and Dunsmuir are quirky, spiritual mountain towns

## Planning Notes

- **Stewart Mineral Springs**: reservations recommended, private tubs available
- **Mossbrae Falls**: short but unofficial trail — park at Hedge Creek Falls and walk the railroad right-of-way carefully
- **Winter access**: Check road conditions on Highway 89 during winter months
- **Spiritual significance**: be respectful at Native American spring sites
