Colorado Hot Springs Packing Checklist

Essentials for any spring, plus add-ons for winter and backcountry trips

Essentials — every trip

  • Swimsuit (plus a spare)
  • Towel or quick-dry microfiber towel
  • Water bottle — 32 oz or larger
  • Water shoes or grippy sport sandals
  • Mineral sunscreen + SPF lip balm
  • Change of clothes and underwear
  • Bag for wet swimsuit and towel
  • Cash and card for entry fees

Food & hydration

  • Salty snacks (trail mix, jerky, chips)
  • Electrolyte drink mix or tablets
  • Substantial snacks for longer days
  • Cooler + ice (summer)

Clothing & layers

  • Light layers + sun hat + sunglasses (summer)
  • Rain shell — July/August afternoon storms (summer)
  • Insulated coat, beanie, gloves (winter)
  • Waterproof slip-on sandals or boots (winter)
  • Microspikes for icy trail approaches (winter)
  • Flip-flops for locker rooms

Backcountry add-ons — hike-in & camping springs

  • Tent, rainfly, stakes
  • Sleeping bag rated 20–30°F
  • Sleeping pad
  • Camp stove + meals + extra snacks
  • Headlamp (red-light mode for shared pools)
  • Water filter
  • Toilet kit: trowel or WAG bag, TP, hand sanitizer
  • Bear-resistant food storage where required
  • Offline maps or GPS
  • First aid kit

Reminders: No glass anywhere. Alternate soaks with water — skip alcohol. Apply sunscreen well before arrival; lotions and oils clog filters and pollute natural pools. Pack out everything you pack in.
