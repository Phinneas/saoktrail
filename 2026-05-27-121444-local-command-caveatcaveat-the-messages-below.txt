⏺ Exactly. It's for when your data lives in multiple different places and you
  want the AI to access all of them through one consistent interface.

  Concrete example:
  - Customer emails in Gmail

  - Product docs in Google Drive

  - Session cache in Redis

  the agent just does ls /gmail/inbox, cat /slack/support/thread-123, grep

  don't want to babysit per-service code.

  one database/warehouse.
